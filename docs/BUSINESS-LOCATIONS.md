# Falmouth After Five business location audit

The 15 existing business profiles now have researched premises locations and fuller addresses. Mangos Sports Bar uses the coordinates supplied for Discovery Quay. Windjammer has a provisional map-provider point because published pins conflict; its exact entrance still needs local confirmation. These are premises markers, not surveyed entrance coordinates or confirmation that a business is participating in the next campaign.

The source review was completed on 9 September 2026. Operator websites and Falmouth BID establish addresses; operator/BID map data was compared with named OpenStreetMap records. Where a BID pin disagreed with stronger evidence, the operator point or a named map feature was preferred. Google map destination coordinates were taken from the actual destination link, not the map viewport centre. Mangos retains the supplied coordinate precision; the extra digits do not imply a survey.

## Findings and remaining uncertainty

**The Shed.** BID venue pin; OSM point agrees within a few metres.

**The Boathouse.** BID venue pin, cross-checked with the mapped pub. OSM uses a different postcode; keep BID address.

**The Games Room.** BID venue pin, supported by the named OSM business nearby; premises point, not an accessibility entrance survey.

**The Orgia.** OSM named venue point; address supported by the indexed former official page. Operating status needs confirmation: Tripadvisor marks it closed and the current domain returned unrelated content.

**Pennycomequick.** BID pin and full address; named OSM point agrees closely.

**Pocketful of Stones.** BID venue pin, supported by OSM. Corrects the old High Street demo address.

**The Poly.** Named OSM arts-centre point, aligned with the official address. Prefer the venue point over the larger building centroid.

**Mangos Sports Bar.** Exact supplied coordinates retained. The Discovery Quay OSM record supports the location; do not use the older Mangos Bar & Dance point on Church/Market Street.

**Windjammer.** Apple Maps named POI used provisionally. BID pin is near Fives; CAMRA gives a point about 70 m away. Correct working-slipway premises area, exact entrance still needs local confirmation.

**Fives Cyderhouse.** Coordinates from the destination in the venue’s own Directions link. Map label retains its former Five Degrees West name; current website confirms Fives at this address.

**Harbour Lights.** BID restaurant pin with official address; OSM distinguishes restaurant and takeaway points nearby.

**The Greenbank.** BID hotel pin agrees with OSM building way 159875075. Keep separate from The Working Boat below it.

**The Grapes.** Operator website’s structured geo and map pin; supported by OSM. Replaces the misplaced BID pin beside Pocketful of Stones.

**Harry’s Taqueria.** Council licensing record supports number 22 Killigrew Street; named OSM restaurant point used. Habanero’s on Arwenack Street is a separate business.

**Maya’s.** Official shop address plus named OSM point. OSM tags it as clothes, consistent with the shop alongside the creative studio.

Windjammer’s published alternatives are BID 50.1509824, -5.0645493; [Cornwall CAMRA](https://cornwall.camra.org.uk/viewnode.php?id=135331) 50.1521000, -5.0635300; and Apple Maps 50.1517129, -5.0642949. Apple also publishes a walking access point at 50.1514554, -5.0643218, distinct from the business point. The operator describes a working-slipway location; Apple’s business point is adopted pending entrance confirmation. No exact entrance claim is made.

The Orgia’s historical address is well supported, but its current trading status is unresolved. [Tripadvisor’s listing](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Restaurant_Review-g186235-d24134262-Reviews-The_Orgia-Falmouth_Cornwall_England.html) marks it closed, while indexed booking material persists. Retain it as an explicitly historical demonstration until Falmouth BID confirms whether it should feature in live content. Its old website should not be promoted as a live booking destination without checking it.

Mangos’ Discovery Quay identity is also supported by [recent public posts mirrored in its business listing](https://www.findglocal.com/GB/Falmouth/1155269464333679/Mangos-By-The-Sea). The old town-centre dance-bar record is a distinct map listing and was not used.

## Data provenance and maintenance

The exact adopted values and source URLs are in `server/demo-locations.json`. `scripts/import-demo-locations.php` updates only the existing demo profiles, in a transaction, retaining photos and offers. It increments record versions so an older staff form cannot silently overwrite the corrections. A private snapshot is saved before changes. New demo databases receive the same researched locations.

OpenStreetMap contributors supply the named map features used for cross-checking and several adopted points, under the [Open Database Licence](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Mapbox already shows OpenStreetMap attribution. Source links above identify individual objects so subsequent edits can be reviewed. Public websites without visible publication dates are recorded by access date; older licensing evidence is labelled by its document date. Location research does not certify opening hours, current offers, or step-free access.
